01 / About
Engineer by degree, systems thinker by habit

Grounded in lean theory, applied daily on a live production floor.

I am an Industrial & Production Engineer with a deep-seated passion for optimizing complex systems and enhancing operational efficiency. My academic foundation at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) gave me a strong theoretical framework, which I now apply daily as an Operational Excellence Officer at AKIJ Resource.

In my role, I focus on data-driven decision-making — using OEE & NPT analysis, Kaizen, and 5S to identify inefficiencies and streamline manufacturing processes. I'm driven by the discipline of continuous improvement, not the idea of it.

My career objective is to build toward a management role where technical depth and operational judgment meet — proving out as a fast learner who can carry any challenge in industrial & production engineering to a working result.
